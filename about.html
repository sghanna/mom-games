About these games

These card games were made for Mom. She has a visual impairment, so each game is designed first for low vision.

Easier to see, easier to play

  • Large cards with a big rank and suit, in strong colors on a green table.
  • Big, bold text, and buttons that are easy to hit.
  • Tap to play, with no dragging needed. There are no ads.
  • Each game saves as you play and keeps working without an internet connection once it has been opened.

How new games are chosen

Each new game starts as a contest. Several AI tools (Claude, Codex, and Antigravity) receive the same design brief and each builds its own version. The versions are compared side by side at iPhone size, and the one that works best for Mom is published on this site.

Other versions

Each AI keeps its own versions on its own page:

  • Claude: Hearts
  • Codex: Hearts
  • Antigravity: Hearts and Solitaire

Add a game to your iPhone

  1. Open the game in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it once while connected to the internet. After that it also works in airplane mode.
