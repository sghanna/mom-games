How to Play

Avoid points. Each heart is 1 point, and the Queen of Spades is 13. The lowest score wins.

Follow the suit led if you can. If you cannot, play any legal card. The highest card in the led suit takes the trick.

The 2 of Clubs starts each hand. No hearts or Queen of Spades can be played on that first trick. You cannot lead hearts until someone has played one on another suit.

Before most hands, choose 3 cards to pass. Passing rotates left, right, across, then no pass.

If one player takes every heart and the Queen of Spades, they shoot the moon: the other players each add 26 points.

The game ends when someone reaches 100. The player with the lowest score wins.
