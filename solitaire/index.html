How to Play

Tap a face-up card to select it. A gold outline shows your choice. Then tap the pile where you want it to go.

Double-tap a card to move it automatically. The game tries a foundation first, then an open tableau spot.

Build tableau columns downward, alternating red and black. Only a King can fill an empty column.

Build each top foundation from Ace to King in the same suit. Tap the deck to draw. When it is empty, tap it again to recycle the cards.

You can use Undo as often as you like.
